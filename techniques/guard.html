QUICK GUIDE

KEY POINTS

  1. At long range, keep the rear hand near your cheek.
  2. Keep the lead hand ready to block or punch.
  3. At close range, raise both hands to cover your head.
COMMON MISTAKE

Do not cover your eyes so completely that you lose sight of the opponent.
