SLIP

Move your head off the punch line.

04 / 05

A slip uses a small side movement to let a straight punch pass.

KEY POINTS

  1. Keep your eyes on the opponent.
  2. Move the head just outside the path of the punch.
  3. Stay balanced and ready to counter.
COMMON MISTAKE

Do not lean so far that you cannot return to your stance.
