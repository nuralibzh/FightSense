ABOUT THE TRAINING

MORE THAN JUST PUNCHES.

Boxing is not only about power. Timing, reaction, movement and technique matter just as much.

FightSense turns your webcam into a training tool. It follows your movements during a session and helps you understand what you are doing well and what needs improvement.
