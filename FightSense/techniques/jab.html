QUICK GUIDE

KEY POINTS

  1. Start from your boxing stance with your hands up.
  2. Extend the lead hand straight toward the target.
  3. Bring the hand straight back to guard.
COMMON MISTAKE

Do not leave your lead hand extended after the punch.
