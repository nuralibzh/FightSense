ABOUT FIGHTSENSE

Boxing training
made accessible.

FightSense is a browser-based boxing training platform created for ADMIT Hackathon 2026. It uses camera-based movement tracking to recognize boxing movements and give users feedback on their technique.

No controllers. No special equipment. Just a webcam and movement.

CREATED BY

Nurali Bolat

PROJECT GOAL

Train anywhere.

Make basic boxing training more interactive and accessible using only a camera and a web browser.

THE IDEA

HOW FIGHTSENSE WORKS

The camera becomes the controller. FightSense reads body movement and turns boxing practice into an interactive training session.

01

CAMERA TRACKING

Detects body position and movement through the user's webcam.

02

MOVE RECOGNITION

Recognizes boxing movements such as Jab, Cross, Guard, Slip and Duck.

03

FORM FEEDBACK

Detects incorrect movement and shows what the user needs to correct.

04

TRAINING LEVELS

Choose Beginner, Amateur or Pro to match your experience.
